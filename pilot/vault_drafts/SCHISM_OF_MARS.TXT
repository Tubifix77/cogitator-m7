RECORD: THE SCHISM OF MARS (Mechanicum civil war during the Horus Heresy)
- Fabricator-General at the outbreak of the Heresy: Kelbor-Hal. He sided with Horus and founded the Dark Mechanicum. CORRECTION: Perturabo was never Fabricator-General; he was the Iron Warriors primarch.
- Kelbor-Hal had opposed the Treaty of Olympus, seeing it as the Emperor subordinating the forges.
- Zagreus Kane: loyalist Forge Master of Mondus Occulam and Fabricator-Locum (Kelbor-Hal's deputy). He refused to join the Dark Mechanicum and led resistance from his forge complex.
- Loyalists were evacuated from Mars with the help of Captain Sigismund of the Imperial Fists (not "Titus").
- In exile, Kane was named Fabricator-General of Mars, but ruled in name only (Fabricator-General-in-Exile) while traitors held Mars. CORRECTION: his title was not "Lord of the Forge".
- Koriel Zeth led the loyalist defence of Magma City, aided by the Titan Legion Legio Tempestus and House Taranis, the oldest Knight House of Mars, which was almost wiped out. (The Imperial Fists are a Space Marine Legion, not a Titan Legion.)
- Lukas Chrom: traitor master of the forge Mondus Gamma, inner-circle founder of the Dark Mechanicum. His Kaban Machine fired the opening shots of the Schism by attacking Adept Ipluvien Maximal's reactor at Gigas Fossae, on Mars (not the Imperial Palace on Terra).
Sources: Lexicanum (Kane, Lukas Chrom); Horus Heresy: Mechanicum.
