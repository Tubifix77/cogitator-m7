RECORD: FORGE WORLD RYZA
- Ryza is famed for plasma technology: plasma weapons and plasma-armed vehicles such as the Leman Russ Executioner and the Stormblade super-heavy tank. Some plasma weapons are now made only on Ryza. CORRECTION: Ryza is not known for bolters, and the Baneblade is not its signature tank.
- Titan Legion: Legio Crucius, "the Warmongers".
- Ork invasions: Waaagh! Grax and Waaagh! Rarguts. Grax was defeated during the 13th Black Crusade; after the Great Rift, daemons invaded too.
- Horus Heresy: the Dark Mechanicum besieged Ryza for its plasma knowledge. The loyalists held, but over four-fifths of the world's infrastructure was destroyed.
Source: Lexicanum (Ryza); Adeptus Titanicus.
