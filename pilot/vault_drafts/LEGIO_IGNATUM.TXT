RECORD: LEGIO IGNATUM, "THE FIRE WASPS" (Titan Legion of Mars)
- Nickname: the Fire Wasps, after an ancient Terran predator that frenzies when its nest is disturbed. CORRECTION: not "the Purifiers".
- One of the Triad Ferrum Morgulus, the three original Titan Legions of the Age of Strife: Legio Ignatum, Legio Mortis and Legio Tempestus. Legio Mortis is its arch-enemy.
- Motto: "Inveniam viam aut faciam" (I will find a way, or make one).
- Colours: red, black and yellow.
- Horus Heresy: broke with the traitor Kelbor-Hal and fought for the Emperor; served in the Belt of Iron and at Urdesh.
- Siege of Terra: its Emperor Titan Imperious Prima, under acting commander Princeps Maximus Cydon, was destroyed by the Legio Mortis Imperator Titan Dies Irae.
- Imperious Prima was later seemingly recovered and served until the Third War for Armageddon, where Orks destroyed it (not at Vraks).
- Two Ignatum Warhound Titans have the honour of guarding the Eternity Gate of the Imperial Palace.
Sources: Lexicanum (Legio Ignatum, Imperious Prima); Warhammer Community "Lore - the Fire Wasps of the Legio Ignatum".
