RECORD: RECENT CAMPAIGNS - ARKS OF OMEN (2023) AND ARCHMAGOS (2025)
- Vashtorr the Arkifane: a daemonic forge-master, demigod of inventors and engineers, of daemon-primarch rank but not a god. He seeks to ascend as the fifth Chaos God and allied with Abaddon the Despoiler.
- Arks of Omen: Abaddon and Vashtorr hunted the fragments of "the Key" (the Key to the Lock), which together unlock "the Weapon". The Arks of Omen are their fortified ships, not the prize.
- Vashtorr assaulted the Rock, the Dark Angels' fortress-monastery. The daemon prince Be'lakor appeared and duelled him to a near stalemate. CORRECTION: not Sanguinius, who died in the Horus Heresy.
- Finale (Arks of Omen: The Lion): Vashtorr seized the Tuchulcha Engine, an ancient warp device with its own alien mind, and carried it to his sanctum on Wyrmwood. The Lion returned in this saga.
- "Archmagos" (Guy Haley, Black Library, late 2025): on Roboute Guilliman's orders, Cawl tries to stabilise the Attilan Gap to open a stable route across the Great Rift into Imperium Nihilus. He needs control codes kept on an ancient Necron world at the event horizon of a black hole. His interference provokes Vashtorr.
Sources: Warhammer Community (Arks of Omen finale recap, 2023; Archmagos announcement, 15 Oct 2025); Wargamer "Who is the Arkifane".
