RECORD: FORGE WORLD STYGIES VIII
- A forge moon orbiting a ringed gas giant in the Vulcanis binary system.
- Home of the Xenarites, a secret Mechanicus sect that studies and exploits xenos technology, a practice most Tech-Priests find offensive. (Not the Dark Mechanicum.)
- Horus Heresy: Stygies VIII nearly fell to rebels and was saved only by the intervention of Eldar (Aeldari) forces, not the Ultramarines.
- Its two Heresy-era Titan Legions, Legio Vulcanum I "Dark Fire" and Legio Vulcanum II "Lords of Ruin", both turned traitor and sided with Horus. The world now houses the Legio Honorum.
- Industry: finest munitions, gun barrels and recoil dampeners; one of the few producers of the Leman Russ Vanquisher.
- In M36 a dozen Deathwatch Kill-Teams (Ordo Xenos) launched a devastating assault on the world over its xenos-tech practices. The High Lords later ruled Stygies VIII too vital to interfere with.
Sources: Lexicanum (Stygies VIII); Warhammer 40k Wiki (Legio Vulcanum I).
