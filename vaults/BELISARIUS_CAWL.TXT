RECORD: ARCHMAGOS DOMINUS BELISARIUS CAWL
- Rank: Archmagos Dominus; honorific title "Master of Masters".
- Age: over ten thousand years. He has been memory-wiped at least twice, so his own origins are a mystery even to him. (No canon source names a novel "The Gatekeepers".)
- He first appeared in the Horus Heresy as a Tech-Acolyte. He holds the Emperor to be the Omnissiah but prefers innovation over ritual maintenance, which makes him a hero to some and a heretic to others.
- Roboute Guilliman, not the High Lords of Terra, charged him after the Heresy with creating the Primaris Space Marines and with restoring Guilliman should the Primarch fall.
- He built a separate lesser copy of himself called the Cawl Inferior (not "Cawl-Prime").
- Novels by Guy Haley: secondary character in the Dark Imperium trilogy; lead in "Belisarius Cawl: The Great Work", "Genefather" (where he matches wits with Fabius Bile) and "Archmagos" (2025).
Sources: Lexicanum (Belisarius Cawl); Warhammer Community, 15 Oct 2025.
