RECORD: THE TREATY OF OLYMPUS (a.k.a. Treaty of Mars, Treaty of Olympus Mons)
- The Mechanicum calls the pact binding Mars to the Emperor the Treaty of Olympus; Imperial sources call it the Treaty of Mars. Dated c. 739.M30 or 798.M30 (sources differ).
- The Emperor promised: protection of Forge World sovereignty and autonomy; the right to keep practising the Cult Mechanicus despite the secular Imperial Truth; and six Navigator Houses for Mars (not 100), whose own Navigators had dwindled.
- Mars provided: arms, a war fleet and its full industrial capacity (starships, vehicles, wargear) for the Great Crusade.
- Many Tech-Priests hailed the Emperor as the Omnissiah because he could seemingly repair machinery instantly with a mere touch.
- Not all accepted it: Kelbor-Hal and others saw the Emperor as a warlord subjugating Mars.
Source: Lexicanum (Adeptus Mechanicus / Martian Mechanicum).
